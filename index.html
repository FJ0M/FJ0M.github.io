About

I combine AI design tools with a physical understanding of atomic interactions to make new proteins and better molecules. My path ran from small-molecule drug discovery at GSK, through the sequence-to-structure rules of α-helical coiled coils, to de novo binders and molecular machines in Copenhagen, and now free energy methods for drug discovery at Schrödinger.

I also make scientific illustrations in Blender and ChimeraX, trying to capture the function and beauty of biological macromolecules.

Now at Schrödinger

Biologics All Access Applications Scientist since September 2025. I apply free energy calculations and physics-based modelling to lead optimisation, run projects in LiveDesign, and present the science at workshops and conferences.
